The following rules represent the Beliefs from the BDI approach at the core of this explainability project.
The rules are directives that are used in the process of plan generation for the robotic helper for the load dishwasher composite task.
The rules have a descending priority, meaning the first rule takes priority over the second one and so on.

The rules are:
1. Small items are loaded before medium items. Medium items are loaded before big items.
2. Items are loaded from left to right.
